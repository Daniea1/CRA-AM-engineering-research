## Recommended structural fixes (highest impact first)

1. *Eliminate §7.13 _or_ rewrite §9 Conclusion* — they cover the same content.
2. *Restructure §7.4–7.8* so that the five Security Gap findings are not restated before each discussion paragraph.
3. *Consolidate "limitations / threats to validity"* into a single section (§7.12), with §3.7, §6.6, and §8's validity paragraph reduced to back-references.
4. *Remove Szedlak's 12.3% statistic* from three of its four current appearances.
5. *Stop restating the merged-taxonomy construction* in §2.3, §2.5, §5.1, §5.2, §5.3, and §7.2 — consolidate construction in §5.2–5.3 and use one-line references elsewhere.
6. *Do not re-quote the Research Questions* in §7.9 — answer them by reference.
7. *Tighten §6.2* so it does not re-describe the survey instrument already specified in §3.
